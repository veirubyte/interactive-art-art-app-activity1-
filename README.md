# The Cost of Color

"Isolation is a slow architecture. Left to our own devices, we progress at a steady, monochrome pace. The self builds the self: stable, predictable, grey.

Community is the disruption of that stability. When others enter our lives, they introduce brilliant, unpredictable hues. They can accelerate our ascent, but they do so with a heavy, uncalibrated velocity. Because we cannot truly know the structural integrity of another's soul, our attempts to connect are often clumsy collisions. To invite color into your life is to invite the very force that might shatter your foundation. The art of living is choosing between the lonely safety of the grey, and the perilous, vibrant collapse caused by others."
